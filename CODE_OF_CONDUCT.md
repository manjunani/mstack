# Code of conduct

mstack is a small project. These are the rules for taking part in its issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Talk about the work, not the person.
- Give evidence for claims: the request you typed, the output you saw, the source you read.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and give a reason when you do the same.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or attacks on anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, advertising, and repeated off-topic posts.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors, including by mass mentions.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. Serious or repeated breaches lead to a permanent block.

To report a problem privately, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
